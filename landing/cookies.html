Cookies & App Storage

Here is what the JengaBiz website stores in your browser and what the Android app stores on your phone. Last updated: 25 November 2026.

1. Website cookie

The JengaBiz website uses a necessary first-party cookie to remember your cookie choices. You can remove it through your browser’s site-data settings; the notice will appear again on your next visit.

The site does not currently use advertising, analytics, or cross-site tracking cookies. A referral code in a page link is displayed for sign-up and is not saved in a cookie.

2. Cookie choices

The banner lets you accept all optional categories, reject all non-essential categories, or manage Analytics and Marketing preferences. Necessary preference storage remains active so we can remember your choice. JengaBiz currently has no analytics or advertising tools enabled, so selecting those optional categories does not turn on a tracker today. Any optional tool added later must follow the saved choice.

Use “Cookie settings” in the footer to reopen preferences. You can also remove the preference cookie in your browser’s site-data settings; the notice will appear again on your next visit.

3. Storage in the mobile app

The JengaBiz Android app does not use browser cookies for sign-in, analytics, or advertising and has no separate cookie banner. During sign-up, accepting the Terms and Privacy Policy also acknowledges essential app storage. The app keeps sign-in information, preferences, and copies of shop records and selected images on the device for convenient access and supported offline use. Notification settings are used to deliver stock alerts you enable.

These are app and device storage, not website cookies. You can change notification preferences in the app and Android settings. Uploaded profile photos can be replaced or removed anytime from Profile. The photo picker shares only the profile or product image you select. Contacts access is requested only when you choose Invite a contact; your address book is not uploaded or saved by JengaBiz.

4. Clearing stored data

Clear the JengaBiz cookie from your browser to reset the website notice. On Android, clearing app storage or uninstalling the app removes local preferences and cached information. Shop information already synced to your JengaBiz account remains on the service, but changes waiting to sync may be lost if the local copy is removed first.

5. Other websites

Links to GitHub, MF Technologies, or other sites open services governed by their own privacy and cookie practices. This policy covers the JengaBiz website and app only.

6. Contact

For questions about cookies or app storage, contact info@mftechnologies.org or call 0748 329 410 / 0712 742 991.
