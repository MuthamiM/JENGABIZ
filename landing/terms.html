Terms of Use

These terms explain the rules for using JengaBiz, the shop management app and its website. Last updated: 23 November 2026.

1. About JengaBiz

JengaBiz is provided by MF Technologies to help shopkeepers in Kenya record and review stock, sales activity, and business information. During sign-up, the required agreement records your acceptance of these Terms, the Privacy Policy, and the essential app storage described in Cookies & App Storage. The native app does not show a browser-cookie banner.

JengaBiz is a recordkeeping tool. It is not a bank, payment service, tax adviser, accountant, or legal adviser.

2. Your account

Provide accurate account and shop details, verify the email address you use, and keep your password, device, and one-time codes private. You are responsible for activity on your account and for limiting access to your shop records.

Tell us promptly if you believe someone has accessed your account without permission. We may take reasonable steps to protect accounts or the service when we detect suspected fraud, abuse, or a security risk.

3. Shop records and product images

You are responsible for the accuracy of product names, categories, SKUs, quantities, costs, selling prices, sales, and other information you enter. Check reports and totals against your source records before relying on them.

You retain your rights to the business information and product images you provide. You must have permission to use each image and must not upload content that is unlawful or violates another person’s rights. You allow JengaBiz to store, display, back up, and transmit that content only as needed to provide the service.

4. Offline use and syncing

The app caches shop information on your phone so you can use supported features when your connection is unavailable. Changes made offline sync when a connection returns. Keep the app installed and its local data intact until pending changes have synced; clearing app data or uninstalling before sync may permanently remove unsynced changes.

Some functions require internet access. Availability can be affected by your network, device, maintenance, or service-provider outages.

5. Reports and decisions

Inventory summaries, revenue totals, sales counts, notifications, and PDF exports are based on the information in your account. They are provided to help you keep records; they do not replace your accounting system or professional advice. You remain responsible for business, purchasing, financial, and tax decisions.

6. Referrals

Refer & Earn is available in Kenya. A new account that enters a valid referral code and verifies its email can award 50 JengaBiz points when the two detected shop locations are in the same county, or 75 points when they are in different counties. Each referred account can qualify once. The app detects the location; users cannot select a county in the referral form.

Points are not cash, and redemption is not currently available. We may remove points associated with duplicate, fraudulent, or abusive accounts.

7. Optional device features

Fingerprint or face authentication is handled by your device operating system. Location access and push notifications depend on your device permissions and app settings. You can change those permissions or preferences at any time; some related features may then be unavailable.

8. Acceptable use and service changes

Use JengaBiz only for lawful business purposes and for information you are authorized to manage. Do not try to disrupt the service, bypass account security, misuse verification codes, or access another user’s records.

We may update or discontinue parts of the service. We will update the date on these terms when they change and provide notice in the app or on the website when a material change needs your attention.

9. Ending use and support

You may stop using JengaBiz at any time. To request account closure or help with your account data, contact MF Technologies using the details below. Some records may need to be retained where required to protect the service or meet applicable obligations.

To the extent permitted by applicable law, JengaBiz is provided as a business recordkeeping service without a guarantee that every feature will be uninterrupted or error-free. Nothing in these terms limits rights that cannot be limited by law.
