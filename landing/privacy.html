JengaBiz policies

Privacy Policy

This policy describes the account, shop, device, and website information JengaBiz uses to provide the service. Last updated: 23 November 2026.

1. Who operates JengaBiz

JengaBiz is operated by MF Technologies. Contact us at info@mftechnologies.org, or call 0748 329 410 / 0712 742 991, for privacy questions and account requests.

2. Information we use

Account details can include your name, shop name, email address, optional phone number, password hash, account settings, and the county recorded for referral eligibility. The password itself is not stored as plain text.

Shop records include product names, categories, SKU or barcode, quantities, prices, costs, sales activity, and product images. Information you add is used to provide the inventory, search, reporting, notification, and export features.

If you request email verification, the service processes your email address and a short-lived one-time code. If push notifications are enabled, the app registers a device messaging token and platform so stock alerts can be delivered to that device.

3. Location and device features

Location permission is optional. When allowed, JengaBiz can use your device location to estimate the nearest supply hub and to detect your country and county for the Kenya-only referral rules. The referral service receives the detected country and county; GPS coordinates are not sent for that referral check.

Fingerprint or face checks are performed by the phone’s operating system. JengaBiz receives the authentication result and does not receive or store your fingerprint or face template. Product photo access is requested when you choose to add an image.

4. How we use information

  • Create and secure accounts, sign users in, and verify email ownership.
  • Store and sync shop profiles, stock records, sales activity, and product images.
  • Show business summaries, generate PDF exports, and send stock alerts you enable.
  • Apply referral rules, prevent abuse, troubleshoot problems, and respond to support requests.
  • Operate, maintain, and protect the website, app, and account service.

MF Technologies does not sell personal information.

5. On-device storage and syncing

The app stores the session token in the phone’s secure storage and keeps shop data, settings, and product image cache on the device for faster access and supported offline work. When online, shop records and images sync to the JengaBiz account database. Product images are limited by the app’s upload rules.

Changes that have not synced remain only on that phone. Clearing app data, removing the app, or losing the device before sync can remove those pending changes. The app does not use browser cookies for sign-in or analytics; see Cookies & App Storage.

6. Service providers

We use service providers to help deliver email verification, push notifications, hosting, and network security. For example, verification emails are sent through Resend and push notifications use Firebase Cloud Messaging. Providers receive information needed for their service and may process it in other countries depending on their infrastructure.

The website does not include advertising pixels, analytics tags, externally loaded fonts, or embedded social-media trackers. Standard web requests may create technical server logs, such as the requested page, time, browser details, and network address, used to operate and protect the service.

7. Retention and account requests

Account and shop information is kept while needed to provide JengaBiz and maintain your account, or for other applicable service and legal needs. Email verification codes expire after a short period and are not retained as active credentials after use or expiry.

To request account closure, access to your information, or help correcting or removing information, email us from the address linked to your account. We may ask you to verify your identity before making changes. Some information may remain where necessary to protect the service or meet applicable obligations.

8. Your privacy rights

You may ask to be informed about the use of your personal information, request access, object to processing, request correction of false or misleading information, or request deletion of false or misleading information. Other rights may apply under the laws that protect you. Contact MF Technologies to submit a request. You may also contact Kenya’s Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

9. Security and policy updates

We use reasonable safeguards such as encrypted connections for public services, password hashing, restricted account access, and device secure storage for session credentials. No online service or device can be guaranteed completely secure. Use a screen lock and do not share passwords or verification codes.

We will update this page when our information practices change and revise the date above. Material changes may also be announced in the app or on the website.
