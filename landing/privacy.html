JengaBiz policies

Privacy Policy

This policy describes the account, shop, device, and website information JengaBiz uses to provide the service. Last updated: 25 November 2026.

1. Who operates JengaBiz

JengaBiz is operated by MF Technologies. Contact us at info@mftechnologies.org, or call 0748 329 410 / 0712 742 991, for privacy questions and account requests.

2. Information we use

Account details can include your name, shop name, email address, optional phone number, sign-in information, account settings, and the county recorded for referral eligibility.

You may optionally upload one profile photo (Personal photo or Shop photo) and replace or remove it from Profile at any time. Shop records include product names, categories, SKU or barcode, quantities, prices, costs, sales activity, and product images. Information you add is used to provide the inventory, search, reporting, notification, and export features.

Email verification and stock alerts require limited account and device information to deliver those features. If you request a suspension review, we collect the explanation you provide and share it with your account details with MF Technologies so the team can assess the request and contact you at your registered email if it qualifies for reactivation.

3. Location and device features

Location permission is optional. When allowed, JengaBiz can use your device location to estimate the nearest supply hub and to detect your country and county for the Kenya-only referral rules. The referral service receives the detected country and county; GPS coordinates are not sent for that referral check.

Fingerprint or face checks are performed by the phone’s operating system. JengaBiz receives the authentication result and does not receive or store your fingerprint or face template. Android’s system photo picker gives JengaBiz access only to a profile or product photo you select (or a photo you take with the camera); the app does not browse your full photo or video library. Uploaded profile photos can be replaced or removed in the app. JengaBiz does not currently use videos.

Contacts permission is requested only when you choose Invite a contact. The selected contact’s display name can personalize the referral message. Your address book is not uploaded to JengaBiz or saved by the app; you choose what to send through Android’s sharing app.

4. How we use information

  • Create and manage accounts, sign users in, and verify email ownership.
  • Store and sync shop profiles, profile photos, stock records, sales activity, and product images.
  • Show business summaries, generate PDF exports, and send stock alerts you enable.
  • Apply referral rules, review support requests, troubleshoot problems, and protect the service.
  • Operate and maintain the website and app.

MF Technologies does not sell personal information.

5. On-device storage and syncing

The app keeps sign-in information, settings, shop data, your profile photo, and product images on your device to support quick access and supported offline work. Uploaded profile photos are stored in your JengaBiz account and TeleDrive backup storage until replaced or removed. Profile photos, product images, and shop changes sync when online.

Changes that have not synced remain on the device and could be lost if local app data is removed before syncing. The app does not use browser cookies for sign-in or analytics; see Cookies & App Storage.

6. Service providers

MF Technologies relies on service providers for communications, hosting, notifications, and related service delivery. They receive information needed for their work and may process it in other countries depending on their operations.

The website does not currently use advertising or analytics tools. Basic technical information about service use may be processed to maintain reliability and protect users.

7. Retention and account requests

Account and shop information is kept while needed to provide JengaBiz and maintain your account, or for other applicable service and legal needs. Email verification codes expire after a short period and are not retained as active credentials after use or expiry.

To request account closure, access to your information, or help correcting or removing information, email us from the address linked to your account. We may ask you to verify your identity before making changes. Some information may remain where necessary to protect the service or meet applicable obligations.

8. Your privacy rights

You may ask to be informed about the use of your personal information, request access, object to processing, request correction of false or misleading information, or request deletion of false or misleading information. Other rights may apply under the laws that protect you. Contact MF Technologies to submit a request. You may also contact Kenya’s Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

9. Security and policy updates

MF Technologies uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information. No online service or device can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your device protected and do not share your password or verification codes.

We will update this page when our information practices change and revise the date above. Material changes may also be announced in the app or on the website.
