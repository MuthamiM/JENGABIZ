About JengaBiz

Made for the people keeping local businesses running.

JengaBiz is a shop management app from MF Technologies. It brings stock records, sales activity, and business summaries into one practical tool for shopkeepers in Kenya.

About MF Technologies

Your records and privacy

Stock and account data are kept in your JengaBiz account and cached on your device for faster access and offline work. Changes made offline sync when a connection is available; unsynced changes can be lost if local app data is removed before syncing.

Fingerprint and face checks are handled by your device. JengaBiz does not receive biometric templates. Location is optional and used for nearby supply information and automatic county detection for referrals.

Answers

Frequently asked questions.

More detail about using JengaBiz and keeping your shop records.
Does JengaBiz work offline?

You can view cached shop data and record supported stock changes while offline. Changes remain on the device until the app can sync them. Keep the app and device storage available until pending changes have synced.

Can I add photos and categories to products?

Yes. Add a product image and choose or create a category when managing inventory. Images are saved with the product and can be uploaded when a connection is available.

How do I receive stock notifications?

Open Preferences in your profile and turn on Push notifications. Android may also ask for permission to show notifications. You can change the app preference or device permission later.

Can I export my stock list?

Use the PDF export option in Stock & Inventory to create a JengaBiz-branded report. Check the exported figures against your shop records before relying on them for accounting or tax decisions.

How does email verification work?

JengaBiz sends a one-time code to the email address you enter during sign-up. Enter that code in the app to verify your address and finish creating the account.

Where can I read the rules and privacy details?

Read the Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, and Cookies & App Storage Policy. Contact us if you need help exercising a privacy request.
